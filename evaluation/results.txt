Question:
What are the principles of admission to taught courses?

Expected document:
Section 3 - Admissions Regulations

Answer:
According to Section 3: Admissions Regulations for Taught Courses, the University will admit students to its courses on the basis of the following principles:

a) reasonable expectation that the applicant will fulfil the objectives of the course and achieve the standard required for the award;
b) the University’s requirements for admission to the course leading to a particular award;
c) equality of opportunity for all applicants.

Additionally, according to Section 2: Statutes and Principles, the University will admit students to its courses on the basis of the following principles:

a) reasonable expectation that the applicant will be able to fulfil the objectives of the course and achieve the academic standard required for the award;
b) the University’s requirements for admission to the course leading to a particular award;
c) equality of opportunity for all applicants.

Question:
What are the regulations for admission to a course with credit or exemption?

Expected document:
Section 3 - Admissions Regulations

Answer:
According to Section 3 of the Academic-regs-2025-26-Section-3-Admissions-regulations-for-taught-courses.pdf, the University will consider admission to a course with credit or exemption on the basis of prior learning and/or prior experiential learning (Section 3.20).

Question 5 (answerable)
======================================================================

Question:
What types of prior learning can be considered for admission with credit or exemption?

Expected document:
Section 3 / Section 4

Answer:
Prior learning that can be considered for admission with credit or exemption includes:

- Certificated prior learning
- Uncertificated prior learning
- Instruction-based learning, provided by any institution, which has not been examined in any of the public examination systems
- Experiential learning from a variety of sources, such as:
  - Within the workplace
  - From training courses, which the individual has completed but which are not certificated
  - Through experience in voluntary work
  - By individual study
