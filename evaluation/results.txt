Question:
What are the regulations for admission to a course with credit or exemption?

Expected document:
Section 3 - Admissions Regulations

Answer:
The regulations for admission to a course with credit or exemption are set out in Section 3 of the Admissions Regulations:

* **3.19** – If the University is satisfied that an applicant has met some of the progression and attainment requirements of a course or pathway through means other than attendance on the planned programme, the student may be admitted to any appropriate point in that course.

* **3.20** – The University will consider admission to a course with credit or exemption on the basis of prior learning and/or prior experiential learning. Such learning may be certificated or uncertificated and will be assessed according to the provisions set out in Section 4 (Recognition of Prior Learning). The credit awarded is subject to the limits on the maximum import of credit outlined in Section 17 (Framework for Undergraduate taught courses) and Section 18 (Framework for Postgraduate taught courses).

======================================================================

Question:
What is Recognition of Prior Learning?

Expected document:
Section 4 - RPL Regulations

Answer:
Recognition of Prior Learning (RPL) is the University of Westminster’s process for assessing and accrediting learning that a student has acquired before entering a degree programme. It encompasses two routes:  

* **RPCL (Recognition of Prior Certificated Learning)** – credit for learning that has been formally certified by a recognised academic institution.  
* **RPEL (Recognition of Prior Experiential Learning)** – credit for learning gained through life experience, work, voluntary activities, or non‑certificated training.  

In both cases the prior learning must be relevant, up‑to‑date (typically within the last five years), and of an appropriate level to be considered for credit toward a Westminster award.

Question 5 (answerable)
======================================================================

Question:
What types of prior learning can be considered for admission with credit or exemption?

Expected document:
Section 3 / Section 4

Answer:
Prior learning that can be considered for admission with credit or exemption includes:

* **Academic prior learning** – any certificated or uncertificated study that has been completed elsewhere and is assessed against the university’s RPL provisions.  
* **Prior experiential learning** – learning gained through work experience, training courses (certified or not), voluntary work, or individual study. This experiential learning may come from any institution or source, including instruction‑based learning that has not been formally examined in a public examination system.  

The university will assess such learning in accordance with the RPL regulations (Section 4) and subject to the maximum credit limits set in the undergraduate and postgraduate frameworks.
